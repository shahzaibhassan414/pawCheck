# Settings screen — content & structure

## Header
- Back button (returns to Home)
- Title: "Settings"

## Account
- Circular profile photo (user-uploadable)
- Account email (shown as entered at signup)
- Subtext: "Pawcheck account"

## Your pets
- List of pets: avatar (initial), name, species
- Row: "+ Add another pet" → opens Add Pet flow

## Preferences
- "Scan reminders" — on/off toggle
- "Units" — Metric / Imperial segmented choice

## Subscription
- "Manage subscription" row (chevron, links to platform subscription management — App Store/Play Store or billing portal)

## Legal
- "Privacy policy" row (chevron)
- "Terms of service" row (chevron)

## Disclaimer note
"Pawcheck gives plain-language guidance, not a diagnosis. It doesn't store or share your pet's medical history with third parties."

## Sign out
- "Sign out" button, destructive style (red text)
